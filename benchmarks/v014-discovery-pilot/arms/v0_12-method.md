# v0.12 crux research method adapter

This is a frozen-corpus projection of the pinned v0.12 `trade-nothing -deepthink2` method. Execute
the method yourself as one researcher; do not claim that separate agents or the full orchestrator
ran. Use only the dispatch and corpus tools.

1. Frame the root question and separate thesis truth, market pricing, and tradeable vehicle. Build
   3–5 decisive cruxes. For each crux state the causal role, pass condition, falsifier, and the
   weakest link that would break the root thesis.
2. Research adversarially. Search for both supporting and disconfirming evidence, including
   substitute explanations, financing or balance-sheet constraints, customer economics,
   regulation, bottlenecks, and timing. Read a source before citing it.
3. Maintain a crux ledger with `SUPPORTED`, `REJECTED`, or `UNKNOWN`. Do not average conflicting
   evidence and do not repair one crux with evidence from another.
4. Harvest at most three OpportunitySeeds from evidence encountered while testing cruxes. A seed
   may come from a surviving or failed crux, but must name its origin crux and its own value-capture
   chain, pricing question, catalyst or observable, falsifier, maturity, and next research step.
   Do not perform a mandatory exhaustive landscape expansion before searching.
5. Conclude the root thesis as `EDGE_FOUND`, `NO_EDGE`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. `NO_EDGE`
   means no demonstrated expectation gap; it never means `AVOID` or `SHORT`.
6. Produce one concise Markdown report containing: root verdict and next action; crux ledger;
   separate thesis/pricing/vehicle judgments; up to three candidate cards; unresolved gaps; and a
   decisive-claim audit table with frozen `doc_id` citations.

Never invent price anchors, evidence, agent isolation, or certainty. Label unsupported fields
`UNKNOWN` and keep leads distinct from investable conclusions.
