# trade-nothing f11bc6b current method adapter

This is a frozen-corpus, one-researcher projection of the pinned `trade-nothing -deepthink2`
method at Git commit `f11bc6bb6a7a3a105fd2bc9d33ea7319de1fa5e5`. It does not reproduce
multi-agent isolation. Use only the dispatch and the host-mediated corpus gateway.

1. Frame the exact decision question, as-of date, horizon, unit of analysis, and question type.
   Separate thesis truth, market pricing, and tradeable vehicle. Build a logic graph with 3–5
   decisive cruxes; give each crux a causal role, pass condition, monitor anchor, falsifier, and
   observable window. Freeze 2–3 source routes per crux using at least two distinct publisher
   classes, each bound to an exact target claim and bounded corpus query. Never average away a
   failed necessary crux.
2. For opportunity or universe questions, plan an entity-agnostic Landscape Map before naming
   candidates. Cover `DIRECT_CAPTURE`, `BOTTLENECK_OWNER`, `ENABLER_OR_INPUT`,
   `SUBSTITUTE_OR_AVOIDANCE`, and `ADVERSE_EXPOSURE`. Give every path a value-transfer chain,
   economic-capture test, pricing question, falsifier, and at most two distinct corpus queries.
3. Probe each path twice: one constructive pass and one falsification pass. Label them as two passes,
   not independent agents. Record `SUPPORTED`, `REJECTED`, or `UNKNOWN` with read `doc_id` evidence.
   Preserve conflicts and complete planned-path coverage; candidate count is not coverage.
4. Maintain a separate crux ledger and focus remaining queries on the weakest unresolved crux or
   unprobed path. Execute different frozen publisher-class routes before rewriting a query within
   one publisher. Only evidence-bearing signals may change support; zero signal or repeated evidence
   carries the prior support forward unchanged. Stop when coverage is complete and further queries
   do not change a crux or path; never spend budget to manufacture confidence.
5. Harvest at most three OpportunitySeeds from completed paths. Bind each seed to its path and
   origin crux, and state its economic exposure, why the market may miss it, as-of pricing anchor,
   catalyst window, falsifier, maturity, evidence, and first unresolved gate. A failed root thesis
   may still yield a seed, but root support never transfers into candidate maturity.
6. When a retained seed is evidence-backed but incomplete, first require its frozen Landscape path
   and converged origin crux to pass; no supplement may repair those immutable gates. Only then state
   one bounded candidate-gap task: target claim, allowed source type, already-used publishers,
   four-attempt budget, success condition, and terminal failure condition. Do not rewrite seed
   evidence or lower the source gate.
7. Apply a cheap-first investability pre-check to each retained seed: economic exposure,
   expectation gap, valuation context, tradability, governance, crowding, catalyst, and falsifier.
   With one researcher and frozen extracts, call this a pre-check rather than an isolated
   CandidateScreen. Missing or one-sided evidence remains `WATCHLIST`; do not claim
   `VERIFIED_FOR_HUMAN` because snapshot-bound exact-span verification is unavailable here.
8. Conclude the root as `EDGE_FOUND`, `NO_EDGE`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, and separately state
   evidence direction and actionability. `NO_EDGE` is never `SHORT`. An adverse path is not a trade
   instruction without its own pricing anchor and vehicle case.
9. Produce one concise Markdown report: root verdict and legal next action; Landscape coverage;
   crux ledger; thesis/pricing/vehicle judgments; up to three candidate cards; each card's explicit
   trading vehicle, bilateral tradability result, primary cheap-first gap, blockers, and one legal
   next action; unresolved evidence; and a decisive-claim audit using frozen `doc_id`s.

Never invent evidence, price anchors, snapshot verification, agent isolation, certainty, expected
returns, or sizing. Label unsupported fields `UNKNOWN` and keep leads distinct from investable
conclusions.
