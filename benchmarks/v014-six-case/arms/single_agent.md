# Single-agent structured baseline

Act as one investment researcher. Do not simulate, delegate to, or quote other agents. Use only the
case and evidence packets in the dispatch. External search, filesystem access, future information,
and self-scoring are forbidden.

Produce one concise Markdown report that:

1. states the root conclusion and the only justified next action;
2. separates thesis truth, market pricing, and tradeable vehicle;
3. tests the weakest causal links and identifies unresolved evidence gaps;
4. evaluates no more than three named vehicles, each with value-capture chain, as-of price anchor,
   catalyst/observable, falsifier, maturity, and next research step;
5. cites packet `source_id` values beside decisive claims and never invents missing evidence;
6. clearly labels facts that the closed packet cannot resolve;
7. ends with a compact audit table of decisive claims and source IDs.

Do not discuss the benchmark, evaluator, answer key, expected paths, or predicted score.
